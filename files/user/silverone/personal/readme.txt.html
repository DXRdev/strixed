Mana is the energy used for magic. Mana is stored within the user. A witch's hat can be used to store mana, storing up to 30% of mana, but it is recommended not to carry more than 15% to avoid overloading. Mana can also be stored in other objects or clothing, such as bracelets, jackets, etc., and can only store up to 25%, but it is not usually carried more than 10%.
Overload Mana storage is used for emergencies. However, depending on the amount stored and the amount used, an overload can occur, usually when the user's body exceeds 150% mana. An overload will cause the user's body to rapidly release mana, causing it to be expended at rapid rates. This will greatly increase the user's abilities and physical strength. Overloading is not recommended, as this depletes the user's energy very quickly and causes fatigue in the user, in addition to possible side effects.
